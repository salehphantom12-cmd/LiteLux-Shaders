# LiteLux Shaders

A lightweight Minecraft shader pack focused on beautiful visuals, high FPS, and smooth performance.

LiteLux aims to improve the vanilla Minecraft experience without turning your GPU into a space heater.

## ✨ Features

- 🌅 Improved sunlight and lighting
- 🌙 Enhanced day/night cycle
- ☁️ Lightweight clouds
- 🌊 Improved water
- 💡 Better block and torch lighting
- 🌫️ Atmospheric fog
- 🌿 Dynamic vegetation
- 🌑 Soft shadows
- ✨ Lightweight bloom
- 🎨 Subtle color grading
- 🔥 Custom Nether atmosphere
- 🌌 Custom End atmosphere
- ⚡ Performance-focused rendering

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

LiteLux follows one simple idea:

> **Vanilla Minecraft, but significantly prettier.**

The shader is designed to improve Minecraft's visuals while keeping performance as a priority.

It avoids extremely expensive techniques such as:

- Path tracing
- Heavy ray tracing
- Expensive global illumination
- Excessive volumetric effects
- Extremely high-resolution shadow maps
- Heavy screen-space effects

Instead, LiteLux uses lightweight approximations and optimized GLSL.

## 🚀 Performance

LiteLux is designed for:

- Gaming laptops
- Mid-range GPUs
- Older GPUs
- High-refresh-rate gameplay
- Players who want shaders without sacrificing too much FPS

An NVIDIA RTX 3060 Laptop GPU should be more than capable of running LiteLux comfortably, depending on Minecraft settings, resolution, render distance, and shader preset.

## 🎛️ Quality Presets

LiteLux provides three quality levels:

### LOW

Maximum performance.

Recommended for:
- Low-end GPUs
- High render distances
- Competitive gameplay
- Maximum FPS

### MEDIUM

Balanced visual quality and performance.

Recommended for most players.

### HIGH

Better lighting, shadows, water and atmospheric effects.

Recommended for more powerful GPUs.
